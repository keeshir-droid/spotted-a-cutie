The Footpath Museum

Every street animal you pass gets catalogued with the straight face of a natural-history museum. The only unserious thing is the label.

Object

Collector
You, for one friend
  1. Tea and cigarette cards: “No. 14 of 50”, three inks, cream borders
  2. Museum labels and herbarium sheets: locality, date, a red accession number
  3. Drugstore photo albums: corner mounts, ballpoint captions
  4. Rubber stamps and postmarks: tilted, speckled, double-ruled
  5. Risograph zines: muted inks, a hair of misregistration
the joke is in the words, never the chrome

Two inks on card stock: the kind ink fills the card inside a printed cream border; a second spot ink (teal for cats) on the ribbon and the damage. Series line, an ink-stamped stat, one move. Nothing under 30 px.

The far-away balcony pigeon, zoomed 2.6×. The personal line replaces the card quote, in the sender’s ink-blue.
